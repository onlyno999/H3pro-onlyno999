# 打斗戏音效与动作精准词汇库 (Fight FX Vocabulary)

## 击打与动作部位词汇
- 击打点：太阳穴 (temple)、下颌偏右侧 (jaw bone)、肋软骨 (ribcage)、横隔膜心窝 (solar plexus)、膝关节侧韧带 (knee ligament)
- 发力动作：拧腰蹬地 (pivot foot thrust)、欺身下潜 (bob and weave)、甩胯摆拳 (hip rotation haymaker)、鞭腿抽击 (roundhouse whip kick)

## 音效声学词汇 (Foley Terms for H3)
- 肉体沉重撞击：`deep thudding flesh impact`, `heavy blunt strike resonance`
- 骨骼受挤压与脆响：`muffled bone crunch crackle`, `joint dislocation pop`
- 破空呼啸：`cutting air whistling sound`, `blade cutting gust`, `rapid whoosh`
- 兵器撞击：`piercing metallic clang`, `metal sparks screeching scrape`
- 倒地与环境粉碎：`violent ground slamming slam`, `wood fracturing smash`, `concrete rubble shattering`
